About

JohnnyBanana Games is an indie videogames development studio. Currently, it's developing a couple of games.

You can know more about us below!

Creation of JohnnyBanana Games
JohnnyBanana Games was created as an indie videogames development studio by José Ángel García Pérez from Spain in 2024, when he started to make games seriously.
What type of games we make
Virtually, anything. Action, adventures, cards, in 2D, in 3D, etc. Also, we use Unreal, Godot and Unity for our games.
Why was JohnnyBanana Games created
Because José Ángel has love and passion for games. Not only videogames, but tabletop games too. And so, he wanted to also make games that others could love to play.
The Future of JohnnyBanana Games
